## What you need to decide

   totals per fill; Paper 1 treats them as per contract. Consequences: net edge +8.93 → +9.58, the vault class
   changes sign, the share of positive H4 cells rises from 49.5 % to 76.3 %, and the bp map F5 has a second unit
   error. Paper 1 needs an addendum and a correction before the final data run. I changed nothing in Paper 1; the
   finding script is reproducible (`scripts/p1_fee_units_finding.py`).
