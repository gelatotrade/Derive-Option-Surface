Date: 25 September 2026. State: branch `paper2-kapital`, commit `3ec74f4` (now `1894bad`; stage E). Synthesis of the findings of nine auditors. Every finding is supported by file:line or a reproducible computation; the auditors' scripts are under `data/p2/audit/<perspective>/` (ignored by git). The auditors changed nothing in the repo; the only exception is this file.

**Key message.** No finding changes any of the four registered verdicts (H1 and H4 rejected, H2 and H3 not rejected). All result files are reproduced bit for bit, H1 to H4 are recomputed independently, and the replica of the three managers agrees with an independent replica from Solidity up to machine precision. The findings mainly concern: the pseudonymisation and the public anchoring of the pre-registration before the first push (A01, A02, A24), the labelling and interpretation in the manuscript (A03, A06 to A14, A18), invalid or too narrow intervals of exploratory quantities (A04, A05), a number check that does not guard against changes (A19 to A21), social cards and GIF (A22, A23), and the finding on Paper 1 (A25 to A27).

**Size of the list.** 69 findings: 1 critical, 26 important, 42 minor. Duplicates among the 90 individual findings of the nine auditors were merged; the column Perspective names all sources.

**State after the correction round.** The correction round of 25 September 2026 is recorded in the Status column of the short list and in the section "Status after the correction round (25 September 2026)", together with the check chain and the independent final check.

## Severity

- **critical**: must be fixed before the first push or any publication, because the damage cannot be undone afterwards.
- **important**: a wrong or misleading statement about data, method or result at a load-bearing place, an invalid statement of uncertainty, a missing disclosure that affects the reading of the pre-registration, or a check that does not do what the documentation claims.
- **minor**: a local inaccuracy, a gap in disclosure without influence on the interpretation, presentation and form.

Departures from the auditors' ratings: A01 raised from three "important" ratings (p1_hygiene twice, prereg) to critical. For merged findings the higher rating otherwise applies (A04, A05, A07, A13, A15, A16), with three exceptions: A34 (form: important, numbers and content: minor), A48 and A49 (figures: important) are minor because they have no consequence for content and interpretation.
