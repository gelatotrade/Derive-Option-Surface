## B2 Capital per fill

Status as of 25 September 2026, pilot cut 17 September 2026 12:00 UTC. Code `derive_surface/capital.py`, tests
`tests/test_p2_capital.py` (22 tests, offline). Data `data/p2/derived/capital.parquet`, plausibility
`results/p2/capital_check.json`. No inference: the numbers below only check the measurement.

### What is computed

- **Capital per fill** as pre-registered: K = p·q − net_IM(q; cash = 0) for the book that holds only the filled
  contract, with q = `maker_side` (+1 maker buy, −1 maker sell) and p = `price`, per contract. In addition the same
  quantity with MM (`K_*_mm`, sensitivity) and the engine's position value `V_*`, so that R = V − net and
  K = p·q − V + R hold.
- **Market state:** `FeedHistory.bulk(ts // 1000, expiry, strike)` with `ts` = taker timestamp from `markouts`.
  The time to expiry counts from the timestamp of the block at the taker second (`block_at_ts`, 2 s per block, so at
  most 1 s before the taker second). That is exactly the state an `eth_call` at this block sees.
- **Parameters:** standard lib per manager from `results/p2/params`, `Timeline.at` at the taker time. The fills are
  grouped by parameter state and computed vectorised per group. The column `reg_*` holds `from_ts` of the entry used
  (−1 = none).
- **Rate (addendum 1, point 2):** PM2 with the PM2 rate feed (`rate`, `rate_conf`), legacy PM with its static feed
  (`rate_pm` = 0, confidence 1), SM without discounting. The stable feed is not loaded, `stable` = 1.
- **Windows:** SM for BTC and ETH over the whole sample, for HYPE from 11 November 2025 00:00 UTC; legacy PM for
  BTC and ETH over the whole sample, for HYPE never; PM2 for BTC and ETH from 12 June 2025 23:00 UTC, for HYPE from
  11 November 2025 00:00 UTC. Outside the window the manager's columns are NaN, and likewise when spot, forward or
  vol at the strike has no value.
- **Columns** (`capital.OUT_COLUMNS`, 39): `trade_id, currency, ts, block, sec, maker_side, option_type, expiry,
  strike, price, amount, index_price, K_sm, K_pm, K_pm2, K_sm_mm, K_pm_mm, K_pm2_mm, V_sm, V_pm, V_pm2, reg_sm,
  reg_pm, reg_pm2`, plus the feed columns `spot, forward, sigma, rate, rate_pm, svi_fwd, spot_conf, fwd_conf,
  vol_conf, rate_conf, fwd_fixed` and the ages `spot_age, fwd_age, vol_age, rate_age` (seconds since `feed_ts`,
  addendum 1, point 3). No account numbers.

### Run

```
python3 scripts/p2_heavy.py --wait-max 500 -- python3 -m derive_surface.capital run --ccy HYPE
python3 scripts/p2_heavy.py --wait-max 500 -- python3 -m derive_surface.capital run --ccy BTC
python3 scripts/p2_heavy.py --wait-max 500 -- python3 -m derive_surface.capital run --ccy ETH
python3 -m derive_surface.capital combine
python3 -m derive_surface.capital check
```

The run writes monthly parts per underlying to `data/p2/derived/capital_parts/v1/` (77 parts) and resumes where
it stopped. For every three months a feed window `FeedHistory(start_ts, end_ts)` is loaded that covers exactly the
fills of the window. Run time according to the log: HYPE 7 s, BTC 42 s, ETH 46 s; largest memory use 3.3 GB (ETH,
`/usr/bin/time -l`). `capital.parquet` has 603,940 rows, one per fill from `markouts.parquet`; `combine` stops
if a fill is missing, duplicated or unknown.

The SM values of the sells match the formula of the contract: K ≈ a·S plus (value − price), with a between
`minSpotReq` and `maxSpotReq`, for BTC and ETH 0.13 to 0.15, for HYPE 0.25 to 0.30
(`results/p2/params/*_sm.json`). The SM buys are the premium.

**Cases with K ≤ 0** (`p1.k_le_0`): IM under SM 10, under legacy PM 9, under PM2 20 fills. All are RFQ fills whose
price lies far from the Paper 1 mark (`mark_b_t`): sells at 10 to 97 times the mark, buys at 0.00002 to 0.09 times
the mark (about 1 USD for an option with a mark of several thousand USD). The pattern fits packages whose total
price is spread unevenly over the legs. K = (p − V)·q + R then turns negative as soon as the gap between price and
value exceeds the requirement. Under MM, R is smaller; that is why there are more cases for PM2 buys there (BTC 142,
ETH 152, HYPE 67). B2 excludes nothing; H2 counts and excludes fills with K_PM2,single ≤ 0 itself, as
pre-registered.

**Comparison with Paper 1** (`p1`): the vol at the strike (`sigma`) equals `iv_mark_t` for 603,938 of 603,940
fills; the two deviations are the two ETH fills with two SVI pushes in the same block from report A1. The SM value
per contract `V_sm / q` deviates from the mark `mark_b_t` by a median 0.0005 bp of the index (p1 −26 bp, p99
+29 bp, `p1.v_sm_minus_mark_bp`). The rest comes from the forward: Paper 1 prices on `SVI_fwd`, the engine on the
forward feed.

**Parameter states** (`regimes`): the fills are spread over 4 SM and 4 legacy PM states for BTC and ETH, 5 SM
states for HYPE, and 20 (BTC), 19 (ETH) and 11 (HYPE) PM2 states. Many PM2 states differ only in
`CollateralParameters`, which have no effect on single contracts without collateral. The first PM2 group applies
from 12 June 2025 22:20:01 UTC, that is 40 min before the window starts.

**Time to expiry** (`sec_min`): at least 1,805 s (ETH), 1,831 s (BTC) and 1,891 s (HYPE). No fill lies in the
30-min settlement window, so `fwd_fixed` is 0 everywhere.

**Feed age** (`ages`, seconds since `feed_ts`; median, p99, maximum): BTC spot 29, 124, 6,084; forward 50, 461,
6,085; vol 62, 260, 6,116. ETH spot 30, 192, 10,366; forward 46, 579, 10,374; vol 57, 321, 10,445. HYPE spot 26,
65, 306; forward 54, 90, 1,100; vol 62, 112, 1,112. PM2 rate in the PM2 window: median 60 (BTC), 53 (ETH), 57
(HYPE). No age is negative. As pre-registered, nothing is excluded because of its age.

### For later tasks

- The key is `trade_id`; cells, net edge and half spread come by join from `markouts` or
  `inference_p1.analysis_frame`.
- `capital.capital_single(mgr, arrays, params, price, is_initial)` and `capital.engine_arrays(fills, feed)` compute
  the same fill with arbitrary parameters (for example for the doses of H4: K_after and K_before with the same market
  state). `capital.manager_arrays` sets the rate confidence to 1 for the legacy PM and reads `rate_pm`.
- Open limits: stable feed not loaded (`stable` = 1, affects only the depeg add-on of the IM); maxExpiries, OI caps
  and account limits have no effect on single contracts.
