## B4 Events, doses and the H4 panel (`derive_surface/p2events.py`)

Status as of 25 September 2026, pilot cut 17 September 2026 12:00 UTC. Implements H4 of the pre-registration
(events, dose, outcome variable, panel). No regression, no placebo draw, no comparison of the outcome variable
between cells or periods: that is stage C.

### Events

Source: `p2params.Timeline(ccy, mgr).changes(keys=...)` with all parameter structures except
`CollateralParameters` and `maxExpiries` (they do not change the capital of a single contract). PM2 (standard lib)
per underlying in the PM2 window (BTC and ETH from 12 June 2025 23:00 UTC, HYPE from 11 November 2025 00:00 UTC),
legacy PM for BTC and ETH in the sample window from 11 January 2024. Changes of the same underlying on the same UTC
day are merged (this affects BTC on 24 May 2026: 04:05:07 and 05:21:47 UTC). Two managers on the same underlying
and day would raise an error; this does not occur. Result: 18 events, exactly those named in the A2 report

The dose is a log ratio; max \|d\| is the maximum over all cells with a dose. Four events drop out under the 1 %
rule: the basis change of the legacy PM on 12 June 2024 (basisContAddFactor 1 → 0.5, MultFactor 1.2 → 2; the
basis contingency practically never binds for a single contract) and confMargin 1 → 0.4 on 10 October 2025 (it
acts only below the confidence threshold, which none of the 10,575 fills of the two dose windows reached: every
dose is exactly 0). HYPE-pm2-20260108 is kept but has only 250 fills in the dose window and no cell with 20 fills
each before and after the event. The decision to keep is robust: with only the cells that have at least 20 valid
fills, the same four events drop out, and the smallest kept maximum is then 0.0195 (HYPE-pm2-20260108). Signs of
the doses (cells with \|d\| ≥ 1 %): on 8 January 2026 capital rises (Static Discount long 1.02 → 0.98, short
0.98 → 1.02; BTC 31 of 33, ETH 38 of 39, HYPE 14 of 14 cells positive); at all other kept events it falls (only
negative cells). Source: `results/p2/h4_doses.csv`, `p2params.describe_change`.

### Dose

- d_{c,e} = mean over the fills of cell c with taker timestamp in [e − 14 days, e) of log(K_after / K_before).
  K = p·q − net_IM(q; cash = 0) with q = maker side (±1), p = fill price, market state from
  `FeedHistory.bulk(ts_ms // 1000, …)`, time to expiry from the block time of the fill (`secToExpiry` as on the
  chain), `margin_pm2.single` with the PM2 rate feed or `margin_pm.single` with the legacy rate feed (`rate_pm`,
  confidence 1). No dependence on `capital.parquet` (B2).
- Parameters: K_before with `Timeline.at(e − 1 s)`, K_after with `Timeline.at(last change of the day)`, each under
  the changed manager. e is the first change of the day.
- Cells: underlying × maker side (`buy`, `sell`) × |Δ| bucket × tenor bucket from Paper 1, label
  `BTC|sell|10-25|7-30d` (`p2events.cell_labels`).
- Scope: 57,151 fills in the 18 dose windows, 57,141 with a valid log ratio, 0 without market state. 10 fills with
  K ≤ 0 under one of the two parameter sets (BTC-pm2-20260108 7, BTC-pm2-20260524 2, ETH-pm2-20260820 1) have no
  logarithm and do not enter the mean; they are counted per cell (`n_nonpos`).
- Small cells with K near 0 (options bought deep out of the money) produce large log ratios, e.g. the HYPE cell
  `buy|00-10|2-7d` on 20 August 2026 with 6 fills and d = −1.648 (K 0.021 → 0.0001 USD). Such cells do not reach the
  panel (at least 20 fills per side).

### Outcome variable and panel

- y_i = 10⁴·hs_i / index_price_i with hs from `inference_p1.analysis_frame` (funding from
  `data/p1/ref/funding_history.parquet` as in Paper 1; hs does not depend on it). Fill key = `trade_id` (unique
  over all 603,940 fills).
- Window: taker timestamp in [e − 14 days, e + 14 days] (both ends included), without the UTC day of e;
  `post` = day after the event day. Cells with a finite dose and at least 20 fills with finite y before and 20
  after the event.
- `data/p2/derived/h4_panel.parquet`: 91,446 rows (36,504 before, 54,942 after), 13 events, 475 cell-event pairs,
  84,919 distinct fills. 6,527 fills appear in two events because windows overlap (ETH 8 and 23 January 2026
  2,861, HYPE 8 and 24 May 2026 2,209, BTC 8 and 23 January 2026 1,457); α_{c,e} separates them. Columns
  `fill_key, ccy, cell, event_id, post (bool), dose, y_hs_bp, day`.
- Only events with `kept = True` enter the panel.

### Plausibility

- Second computation path: 150 capital values (15 random fills × before/after × 5 events, both managers, all
  underlyings) via `FeedHistory.state_at` and `margin_*.net_margin` on a `Book` instead of `bulk` and `single`:
  median relative deviation 0, maximum 5.9e-6 (time to expiry from the taker second instead of the block time, at
  most 1 s). Source: `data/p2/derived/h4_doses/crosscheck.json`.
- A pure collateral change gives a dose of exactly 0 in the test; the direction of the doses follows the parameter
  changes (see above).
- Run time: doses per underlying 7 to 75 s under `scripts/p2_heavy.py`, peak 3.2 GB (ETH); panel 3 s, 0.9 GB.

### For stage C

- `p2events.load_frame()` returns per fill `fill_key, ts, ccy, cell, day, y_hs_bp` (and the contract fields).
- `p2events.load_doses(p2events.event_list())` returns the doses of all events and cells (authoritative are the
  Parquet files under `data/p2/derived/h4_doses/`; `results/p2/h4_doses.csv` is the same table up to rounding in the
  last bit). `p2events.dose_vectors(doses)` → `{event_id: Series(cell → dose)}`.
- `p2events.build_panel(frame, ccy, event_ts, doses, event_id, window_days=14, min_fills=20)` builds the panel for
  any date (placebos too, `event_ts` in seconds) and any dose vector with the same window and cell rule.
- `results/p2/events.csv` contains all 18 events with `kept`; whether the dropped events count for the 28-day
  distance of the placebo dates is decided by stage C.
- CLI: `python3 -m derive_surface p2 events {events | doses [--ccy X] [--max-seconds S] | panel}`; `doses` only via
  `scripts/p2_heavy.py`, resumable (one file per event).

### Readings where the pre-registration is silent

1. Time e of a merged event = first change of the day; K_after with the state after the last change of the day.
2. The dose window [e − 14 days, e) also contains the fills of the event day before e (literally as in the
   pre-registration); the panel leaves out the whole event day.
3. Fills with K ≤ 0 before or after the event do not enter the dose (10 fills, counted).
4. "Over all cells" in the 1 % rule = all cells with at least one valid fill in the dose window.
5. Legacy PM with rate feed confidence 1 (its own feed, constant since deployment); for the two legacy events there
   was no PM2 rate feed yet anyway (deployment 9 June 2025, `p2feeds.FEEDS`).
