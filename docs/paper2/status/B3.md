## B3 Marginal cost, netting value, holding time

Status as of 25 September 2026, pilot cut 17 September 2026 12:00 UTC. Code `derive_surface/books.py` (part 2),
tests `tests/test_p2_books.py` (16 new tests, offline, red first). Data `data/p2/derived/marginal.parquet`,
`data/p2/derived/maker_days.parquet`, `data/p2/derived/holding_fills.parquet`, result
`results/p2/holding_time.csv`, check figures `data/p2/derived/{marginal,maker_days,holding}_check.json`. No RPC
request, everything from existing data. No inference: below are only the scope and the checks of the measurement,
no distributions or medians of the ratios and no comparisons between managers.

Call (each resumable, until `DONE`): `python3 scripts/p2_heavy.py --wait-max 500 -- python3 -m
derive_surface p2 books marginal --max-seconds 400`, likewise `... books maker-days`; `... books holding` needs no
FeedHistory run (12 s, 1.1 GB).

### Marginal cost (H2): `marginal.parquet`

- **Population** (`books.h2_population`): maker rows (`maker_sub`) of the ten dominant subaccounts, fill time
  `ts` in the PM2 window of its currency, and the account's manager in the snapshot at the start of the UTC day of
  its own row (`ts_maker`) is PM2. Result: 100,995 fills of M3 (PM2:HYPE, 32,561), M5 (30,109), M8 (21,707) and
  M10 (16,618). Of the 101,001 maker fills of these four accounts, 6 drop out: M3 on 10 November 2025, the day the
  account was opened, before the HYPE PM2 window and without a snapshot at the start of the day.
- **Sample** (`books.h2_sample`): population sorted by (`ts`, `trade_id`), then
  `np.random.default_rng(20260924).choice(100995, 20000, replace=False)`, rows in population order.
  20,000 fills: M3 6,405, M5 5,958, M8 4,296, M10 3,341; ETH 13,595, HYPE 6,405; first day 4 September 2025, last
  17 September 2026.
- **Book before the fill** (addendum 1): snapshot at the start of the day plus every `BalanceAdjusted` of the
  account up to before the transaction of the fill (`replay_balances_many`, day via `OnchainBooks.locate`), options
  and perps, cash 0, perp with entry price equal to the perp price. Expired options drop out at the block time of
  the taker second. The day of the transaction matches the day of the maker row for all 20,000 fills; for 8,236
  fills (RFQ) the maker row is older than the taker row. Book size before the fill: median 188 legs and 9 expiries
  (columns `n_legs_before`, `n_expiries_before`).
- **Market state and parameters** as in B2: feeds at the taker second (`FeedHistory.state_at`), time to expiry
  from the timestamp of the block of this second, PM2 with the account's lib at the taker second
  (`p2params.account_lib` via the labelled overrides). Override libs take effect from their date: M3 5,365, M5
  3,515, M8 68, M10 2,024 fills with the account lib, the rest with the standard lib.
- **Quantities per fill** (`books.fill_marginal`, `books.marginal_capital`): `dK` = p·q + net(before) −
  net(after) under PM2 (IM) for the whole fill (q = `maker_side`·`amount`), `dK_per_contract` = dK / amount,
  `dK_mm` (MM), `dK_unit` (one contract in the fill's direction), `dK_tape` (tape book from `book_before_fill`,
  sensitivity, available for all 20,000 fills). `K_single_pm2(_mm)` comes from `capital.parquet` (B2), `ratio` =
  dK_per_contract / K_single_pm2, plus `ratio_unit`, `ratio_tape`, `ratio_mm`; NaN if K_single ≤ 0 (1 fill,
  `marginal_check.json`).
- **Checks** (`marginal_check.json`): all 20,000 fills with status `ok`, all quantities finite. The single-contract
  capital computed with the same book engine on the empty book (`K_single_book`) matches B2 exactly at all 20,000
  fills (largest deviation 0.0 USD for IM and MM). For the 2 fills with an empty book before the fill,
  `dK_per_contract` equals `K_single_book` (deviation 0.0).
- **Columns:** `fill_key` (= `trade_id`), `subaccount` (raw, only in `data/p2`), `label`, `day` (UTC day of `ts`,
  cluster for the bootstrap under addendum 3), `ts, ts_maker, block_ts, ccy, manager, lib, expiry, strike, is_call,
  maker_side, amount, q, price, chain_day, n_legs_before, n_expiries_before, gross_before, perp_before, n_legs_tape,
  perp_tape, tape_ok, status, net_before, net_after, dK, dK_mm, dK_unit, dK_tape, K_single_book, K_single_book_mm,
  K_single_pm2, K_single_pm2_mm, dK_per_contract, dK_mm_per_contract, dK_tape_per_contract, ratio, ratio_unit,
  ratio_tape, ratio_mm`.
- Run time: 24 parts (currency × month) in two runs, about 4.5 min together, peak 2.6 GB under the lock.

### Netting value (H3): `maker_days.parquet`

- **Maker-days** (`books.maker_day_list`): (account, UTC day) with at least one maker fill in the Paper 1 sample
  (day of the maker row), start of the day in the PM2 window of BTC and ETH (from 13 June 2025) up to 17 September
  2026: 1,968 account-days. Of these 1,943 computed, 24 without an option position in an underlying with an open PM2
  window (status `no_options`, 20 of them M2) and 1 without a snapshot (M3, 10 November 2025, status
  `no_snapshot`). Days computed per account: M1 120, M2 328, M3 303, M4 203, M5 374, M6 119, M7 10, M8 239, M10
  247; M9 has no maker-day in the window (`maker_days_check.json`).
- **Book:** snapshot at the first block of the day (00:00:01 UTC), options and perps, cash and collateral left out,
  only underlyings with an open PM2 window (`books.filter_pm2_window`: BTC and ETH from 12 June 2025 23:00 UTC,
  HYPE from 11 November 2025; all other currencies drop out).
- **Capital** (`books.maker_day_capital`): K = Σ M_b·q − net(q; cash = 0), M_b = Black-76 on the last pushed SVI
  curve with `SVI_fwd`, D = 1 (`books.mark_b`, checked against `markpath.mark_from_svi`). SM, legacy PM and PM2
  computed separately per underlying and summed; SM and legacy PM with the standard lib, PM2 with the account's
  lib. Legacy PM only with BTC and ETH legs, `strict_expiries=True`, NaN on `TooManyExpiries`. IM and MM
  (`K_*_mm`), per underlying also `K_{mgr}[_mm]_{CCY}` and `premium_{CCY}`, plus `n_legs, n_legs_pm,
  gross_contracts, perp_gross, n_expiries_max, manager, status, pm_too_many_expiries, pm2_over_max_expiries`.
- **Checks:** all 1,943 days with finite K_sm, K_pm2 and their MM counterparts. K_pm is NaN on 303 days, exactly
  the days without a BTC or ETH leg (M3, HYPE only). `TooManyExpiries` did not occur (at most 12 expiries; legacy
  PM allows 18 since 25 September 2024), and PM2's `maxExpiries` was never exceeded.
- Run time: 16 monthly parts in one run, 210 s under the lock.

### Holding time (sensitivity): `results/p2/holding_time.csv`

- **Procedure** (`books.fifo_account`, `books.FifoInventory`): FIFO per account and instrument over all tape
  fills (maker and taker rows) of the ten accounts from 11 January 2024. Before the fills of a day the inventory is
  set to the snapshot at the start of the day: differences are transfers (trade module, liquidation) and close the
  oldest lots (`transfer`, closing time = start of the day, so at most 24 h late) or open lots of unknown origin.
  Lots close through later fills, at expiry or by transfer; open at the pilot cut means censored. Maker rows open
  lots with their `trade_id`.
- **Per fill** (`books.fill_holding_times`, `holding_fills.parquet`): opened quantity and quantity-weighted mean
  holding time in days over the closed parts, with and without transfers.
- **Per cell** (`books.holding_cells`): underlying × maker side × |Δ| bucket × tenor bucket from Paper 1, windows
  `all` and `pm2`; `median_days` over opening fills, `median_days_w` as the lower quantity-weighted median over the
  closed parts, `median_days_excl_transfer`, shares of the opened contracts by type of closing. The file contains
  no account IDs.
- **Scope** (`holding_check.json`): 343,030 maker fills of the ten accounts, all found in the tape; 268,769 maker
  rows open contracts (268,635 of them in the Paper 1 sample). Opened 24.43 million contracts: closed by fill 7.87
  million, at expiry 14.49 million, by transfer 0.50 million, censored 1.57 million. The sum of the parts equals the
  opened quantity. 415 rows (208 `all`, 207 `pm2`), of which 149 and 127 respectively with at least 200 fills.

### Open points for the orchestrator and stage C

1. **dK per fill or per contract:** under the pre-registration ("book after the fill"), `ratio` is the whole fill
   per contract divided by K_single; `ratio_unit` measures the next single contract. Both are available.
2. **SM account limit:** on 1,431 of the 1,943 maker-days the book holds more than 63 options. An SM account on v2
   takes at most 63 options (`get_margin_semantics.md`, section 2). K_sm of these books is therefore the
   counterfactual capital of a book that could not be held in this form under SM. Not enforced; state it in the
   paper.
3. **Legacy PM and HYPE:** K_pm covers only BTC and ETH legs. On days with HYPE legs (M2), K_pm is not on the same
   set of legs as K_sm and K_pm2; the columns per underlying allow the comparison on the same basis.
4. **Exclusions under the pre-registration** (K_PM2,single ≤ 0 for H2, K_PM2 ≤ 0 for H3) are counted by stage C;
   the files contain all rows.
