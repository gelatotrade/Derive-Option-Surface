## B5 Surface, capital surface, reference book, animation (`derive_surface/p2surface.py`)

Status as of 25 September 2026. Surfaces and animations are exploratory under the pre-registration. This file
contains only capital data and plausibility checks, no inference and no comparisons of cells or hypothesis
quantities. Tests: `tests/test_p2_surface.py` (29 tests, offline on a synthetic feed history and the committed
parameter timelines under `results/p2/params`).

### Interfaces

- `ChainSmile(expiry, T, forward, svi, svi_fwd)`: an on-chain SVI curve as a `Smile` (modelled on
  `StickyStrikeSmile` in `shock.py`). The vol at strike K is exactly `LyraVolFeed.getVol` (`chainfeeds.svi_vol`
  with the curve's `SVI_fwd` and `SVI_refTau`), the total variance uses the true time to expiry, `F` is the
  forward feed of the expiry, and `dw_dk` is analytic (0 where the feed clamps the moneyness or caps w).
- `live_expiries(hist, ts, max_age=86400, min_days=0.5)`: expiries more than half a day after ts whose forward and
  SVI curve were signed at most one day before ts.
- `surface_from_state(state)`, `chain_surface(ccy, ts, hist=None, state=None) -> Surface` (from
  `FeedHistory.state_at`).
- `capital_grid(ccy, ts, manager, side, hist=None, state=None, params=None, x=None, tenors=None, n_tenors=24,
  option="otm", is_initial=True) -> DataFrame` with `manager, side, ts, delta, tau, tenor_days, forward, strike, iv,
  k, rate, rate_conf, vol_conf, fwd_conf, is_call, spot, spot_conf, price, net, mtm, K, K_per_forward_bp`.
  `manager` from `sm, pm, pm2`, `side` from `short/sell, long/buy`. Outside the pre-registered window
  `ValueError`.
- `reference_book_series(ccy, days=None, hist=None, timelines=None) -> DataFrame`, `reference_days(ccy)`,
  `run_reference_book(ccy, parts_dir, max_seconds)` (resumable, one part per quarter),
  `combine_parts(parts_dir, out)`.
- `param_events(ccy, managers)` (changes relevant to capital via `Timeline.changes(keys=...)`, without
  `CollateralParameters` and `maxExpiries`, only in the window), `render_frame(grids, ccy, ts, side, series, events,
  ...) -> RGB array`, `save_frame(...)`, `fit_limits(frames)`, `animate(ccy, days, out, hist=None,
  managers=("pm2","sm"), side="short") -> Path` (GIF, MP4 if `imageio` is available), `reference_series_bp(ccy)`,
  `probe(ccy, day)`, `manager_available(ccy, mgr, ts)`, `WINDOW_START`, `MANAGER_COLORS`, `colormap()`.
- CLI: `python3 -m derive_surface.p2surface probe|refbook|refbook-combine|animate ...`. Everything that loads feeds
  with SVI runs via `scripts/p2_heavy.py`. The wiring as `p2 surface` in `p2cli` is still missing (orchestrator).

### Decisions

1. **Price at the grid node:** p is the Black-76 mark on the node's forward with D = 1, that is the valuation of
   SM and legacy PM. For an SM short this gives exactly K = min(a·S, max-loss branch) with
   a = max(maxSpotReq − otm, minSpotReq) (test `test_capital_grid_sm_short_equals_a_times_spot`); under PM2,
   K = R − p·(1 − D). K = p·q − net_IM(q; cash = 0) per contract as in the pre-registration.
2. **Grid:** call delta × tenor from `surface.py` (`DELTA_AXIS`, total variance linear in T at fixed delta), OTM
   option per node (call if K ≥ F). Tenors outside the listed expiries drop out. Between two expiries, forward and
   PM2 rate are interpolated linearly in T, and the confidences take the minimum of the two neighbours. On a listed
   expiry, vol, forward, rate and confidence are exactly the feed values; there `capital_grid` agrees with
   `net_margin` of the respective replica on the single-contract book (test over all three managers and both sides,
   relative 1e-9).
3. **Rate:** PM2 with the PM2 rate feed and the standard lib, legacy PM with its static feed (`rate_pm` = 0,
   confidence 1), SM without discounting (addendum 1, point 2).
4. **Reference book:** every day at 08:00 UTC a short straddle with strike = forward of the expiry whose time to
   expiry is closest to 30 days (tie: the earlier one), 1 contract per leg, both legs at the Black-76 mark with
   D = 1 (call = put). The strike is the ATM forward and not a listed strike, so that the series has no jumps from
   the strike grid. `K_<m>_prev` computes the same book in the same market state with the parameters that applied
   24 h earlier (pure parameter effect). Capital is NaN outside the manager windows (PM2 BTC/ETH from 12 June 2025
   23:00 UTC, that is from the book of 13 June 2025; HYPE PM2 and HYPE SM from 11 November 2025; legacy PM for HYPE
   never).
5. **Colours:** managers fixed in Okabe-Ito slots from `figstyle.PALETTE` (PM2 #0072B2, SM #D55E00, legacy PM
   #009E73) and also distinguishable by line style; capital on the sequential map `cividis` (luminance monotone,
   test `test_palette_follows_figstyle_and_the_colour_ramp_is_greyscale_safe`). Height of the surface = IV.

### Reference book (`results/p2/reference_book.csv`)

- 2,273 rows, 28 columns: BTC 981 and ETH 981 days (11 January 2024 to 17 September 2026), HYPE 311 days
  (11 November 2025 to 17 September 2026). No day is missing, and every day had a live expiry. Parts under
  `data/p2/surface/refbook/{CCY}_{YYYY}Q{q}.csv` (26 files). Run time under `p2_heavy.py`: BTC 55 s, ETH 45 s,
  HYPE 7 s, peak 3.3 GB (`/usr/bin/time -l`).
- Actual tenor of the straddle: 21 to 36 days, median 26 days (column `tenor_days`), because between the weekly
  expiries and the next monthly expiry there is often no expiry listed near 30 days. When the expiry changes, the
  tenor jumps; mark this in figure F5.
- Feed age: on 15 March 2024 at 08:00 UTC, forward and SVI for BTC and ETH were 27,649 and 27,680 s old (the same
  outage for both), otherwise at most 6,200 s (January and February 2024); HYPE at most 126 s (columns `fwd_age`,
  `vol_age`). No exclusion, as pre-registered.
- Plausibility of the levels, K in bp of the forward (1e4·K/forward, minimum / median / maximum over the days in the
  window):

  | | SM | legacy PM | PM2 |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | BTC | 2,724 / 2,945 / 3,000 | 1,888 / 1,950 / 2,700 | 933 / 1,369 / 1,464 (462 days) |
  | ETH | 2,726 / 2,956 / 3,000 | 1,909 / 2,101 / 2,727 | 1,078 / 1,422 / 1,589 (462 days) |
  | HYPE | 5,750 / 5,990 / 6,000 | not available | 1,997 / 4,062 / 4,375 (311 days) |

  As expected, SM lies at about 2·a·S: upper bound 2·maxSpotReq = 3,000 bp for BTC and ETH (0.15) and 6,000 bp for
  HYPE (0.30), slightly below because the call strike lies above the spot and bp refer to the forward.
- Pure parameter effect (days with K ≠ K_prev, relative change K/K_prev − 1): legacy PM 23 February 2025 BTC
  −16.9 %, ETH −17.2 %. PM2 BTC 13 June 2025 −0.35 %, 9 January 2026 +1.6 %, 23 January 2026 −10.0 %, 24 May 2026
  −7.5 %, 21 August 2026 −22.9 %; PM2 ETH 13 June 2025 −0.69 %, 9 January 2026 +1.6 %, 23 January 2026 −4.8 %,
  24 May 2026 −3.3 %, 21 August 2026 −16.7 %; PM2 HYPE 9 January 2026 +1.6 %, 9 May 2026 −7.2 %, 24 May 2026
  −31.2 %, 21 August 2026 −18.4 %. SM never. Further parameter changes (legacy PM 12 June 2024 basis and
  25 September 2024 maxExpiries, PM2 10 October 2025 confMargin, all collateral changes) switch the parameter entry
  (`<m>_param_ts` ≠ `<m>_param_ts_prev`) but change the capital of this book by 0.00 %, because the respective term
  does not bind for an ATM straddle. This is a description of the reference book, not the dose of H4.

### Open points

- MP4: this machine lacks `imageio` and `ffmpeg`; `animate` then writes only the GIF (`animate.write_mp4` skips
  with a warning). For `docs/media` MP4s as in Paper 1, use a machine with both.
- The reference straddle has no fixed tenor node (21 to 36 days). A variant with a synthetic 30-day node would be
  possible at once via `capital_grid(tenors=[30/365])`, but it is not pre-registered.
- `p2cli`: wire the subcommand `surface` to `p2surface.main` (orchestrator).
- Final data run: `END_DAY` in `p2surface.py` is the pilot cut 17 September 2026. For the final data run, raise
  `END_DAY` and empty `data/p2/surface/refbook/` (otherwise the quarterly part 2026Q3 with the old end stays in
  place), then rerun `refbook` per underlying and `refbook-combine`.
- Test suite at the end of this task: 605 passed, 2 failed; both failures are in `tests/test_p2_validate.py` (B1,
  in progress in parallel). Without this file, 586 passed.
