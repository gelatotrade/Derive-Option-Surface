## B1 Validation of the replica against eth_call (gate of the pre-registration)

Status as of 25 September 2026, pilot cut 17 September 2026 12:00 UTC. Code `derive_surface/p2validate.py`, tests
`tests/test_p2_validate.py` (21 tests, offline; fixture `tests/fixtures/p2/b1_chain_cases.json` with 15 real
chain cases, generated by `tests/fixtures/p2/gen_b1_fixture.py`). Results `results/p2/validation.csv` (1,754
rows) and `results/p2/validation_summary.json`, report `docs/paper2/VALIDATION.md`. No inference.

### Result

- **Threshold met in every cell** (underlying × manager, single contracts and books, IM and MM). Single contracts
  under IM over all cells: median |rel| 8.7·10⁻¹⁰, p95 1.4·10⁻⁸, maximum 8.9·10⁻⁸ (799 valid cases); books:
  2.2·10⁻⁹, 1.3·10⁻⁸, 3.4·10⁻⁸ (77 book-manager cases). Threshold: median < 10⁻³, p95 < 10⁻². No addendum needed.
- Spot and forward of the chain are bit-identical to `state_at` in all 1,598 single-contract rows. The remaining
  deviation comes from the vol at the strike (median 1.2·10⁻⁸, at most 2.3·10⁻⁷ relative), because the SVI
  parameters from Paper 1 are stored as float32.
- The vectorised path of B2 (`FeedHistory.bulk` + `margin_*.single`) gives exactly the same as `net_margin` in all
  1,598 rows.
- One case is missing: HYPE PM2 at block 36,366,166 (5 March 2026 23:39 UTC), `getSpot` reverts (spot signed 489 s
  before the block), and the chain computes no margin. The replica keeps computing with the old value (note for
  B2: the age is in `spot_age`).

### Procedure (short)

- Chain side without our data: synthetic account 2⁶⁴ + 20260924 via an `eth_call` state override of `SubAccounts`
  (slots 14 `balanceAndOrder` and 15 `heldAssets`, checked with `eth_getStorageAt`), then `getMargin(account,
  isInitial)` of the manager (`StandardManager`, `PMRM`, `PMRM_2`); the perp part is subtracted via
  `getUnsettledAndUnrealizedCash`. Large PM books (13 cases, 75 to 245 legs) via the single scenarios
  `getMarginAndMarkToMarket(account, isInitial, j)` and the minimum; bit-identical to the direct call on 6 books.
- Single contracts: 100 random blocks per cell (at least 48 pre-registered), seed 20260924 with its own
  `SeedSequence` branch per cell; a fill of the same UTC day from the tape, an expiry with a forward push ≤ 1 h and
  a vol push ≤ 20 min, random side, p = fill price.
- Books: 20 maker-days from the H3 population (2,134 days), 26 books (per day and underlying) under PM2, SM and
  legacy PM (BTC, ETH), p = mark M_b. Accounts as labels from `p2ids` (M1 to M6 and M8).
- RPC: 1,997 requests, ≤ 2/s, log `data/p2/logs/B1.jsonl`; raw data `data/p2/validation/`.

### Interfaces

- `p2validate.keccak256(bytes)`, `selector(sig)`; `account_state_diff(account, balances)`,
  `state_override({account: balances})` with `balances = [(asset, subId, 1e18-int)]`; `book_balances(ccy, book)`,
  `snapshot_book(snapshot_rows, ccy, ts) -> (balances, Book)`.
- `replica_net(mgr, book, state, params, is_initial) -> float` (NaN if the chain rejects the book: more expiries
  than `maxExpiries`, or a feed value is missing); `mark_premium(book, state)`; `capital`, `rel_err`.
- `chain_single(rpc, case)`, `chain_book(rpc, case, per_scenario=False)`; `summarize(df)`.
- CLI: `python3 -m derive_surface.p2validate {plan --ccy X | chain [--retry-failed] | check-scenarios | report}`;
  `plan` loads a full `FeedHistory` with SVI and runs only via `scripts/p2_heavy.py`.

### Open points

- The oracle contingencies (confidence ≥ 0.95 at every block, threshold 0.55) and the depeg add-on (stable
  0.99938 to 1.00144, threshold 0.99) were never active; they are checked only via the v2-core reference cases
  (A3, A4).
- The standard lib is checked. For MM in a maker book the account's override lib applies (only mmFactor differs).
