**Goal:** Eight figures in the main text plus one in the appendix that carry the paper's theory and findings, so that the prose can be cut down to what the figures do not show. Every figure is produced from tested aggregations, builds in the CAS layout and agrees with the numbers sheet.

**Architecture:** `derive_surface/figures_p1.py` holds a registry `FIGURES` with one function per figure; each function receives the analysis frame, the result files and an output directory and returns the paths it wrote. Drawing uses `figstyle` (CAS widths, fixed class colours), computation uses `figdata` (median cluster bootstrap, delta-tenor matrix, weekly series, waterfall). CLI `p1 figures`. A check script compares the numbers in the figures against `results/p1`.

**Tech Stack:** Python 3.9.6, matplotlib 3.9 (Agg), numpy, pandas; pytest; tectonic for building the figures into the manuscript.

**Basis:** Design workflow of 2026-09-18 (three perspectives: mechanism, empirics, practitioner; two judges: referee, design). The selection per slot is in Task 2, the rationale for the merges in `docs/paper1/FIGURE_SELECTION.md`.

## Global Constraints

- The constraints from Plans 1 to 3 continue to apply (Python 3.9, no new dependencies, tests offline, local commits with trailer).
- Widths: single-column 3.4 inches, two-column 7.0 inches; font 8 pt; output PDF (manuscript) and PNG (inspection) to `paper/figures/`.
- Colours only from `figstyle.PALETTE`; classes always in `figstyle.CLASS_COLORS`; every figure must remain readable in greyscale (shape, line style or hatching in addition to colour).
- Every figure is understandable without its caption: axes with units, sign convention stated, sample size visible.
- No number in a figure that does not come from `data/p1/derived/markouts.parquet` or `results/p1/`; the check script compares core numbers against `results/p1/summary.json`.

## Conditions from both judges that apply to every figure

- Next to every class interval stands the wild cluster p-value against zero. The two conspicuous classes lie at
  0.1003 and 0.1013, that is above five percent, while their percentile intervals exclude zero. Addendum 2,
  item 4 requires both; a figure that shows only the interval claims more than the inference supports.
- No font below 7 pt. At most one number per grid cell; everything else becomes frame style or a marker.
- No dispersion drawn as a hundred hairlines with alpha 0.15. Dispersion becomes a p25/p75 band, that is one object
  instead of a hundred, otherwise it disappears in four-colour print or produces moiré.
- Symlog axes carry the linear core as a shaded strip, are labelled on both sides and state in the axis title that
  each decade has the same area. Without this marking the axis is not used.
- No hidden second axis. A conversion note in the plot area is a second axis and is dropped.
- Classes carry their cluster count G (17 / 33 / 8 / 104 / 4,512 / 10,749); classes with G below 40 are shaded.
- Cells below 200 fills remain as an empty cross instead of silently missing.
- Every encoding must hold up in greyscale: marker, line style or hatching in addition to colour.

#### T1: Anatomy of a fill (two-column, 2.8 in)

- **Data:** one fill from `example_fill`, the matching on-chain curves from `curve_at`, and as dispersion the 100
  nearest fills in the same cell as a p25/p75 band. Example from the pilot sample: BTC-20260807-64000-C on
  2026-08-01, the maker sells at 496.68 against a mark of 473.98 (half spread +22.70), 30 minutes later the mark
  stands at 669.46 (markout -172.78). Curve age at the fill 25 s.
- **Panel A:** price against time, step shape, the three marks as points, path (a) drawn offset at its actual
  distance. To the right a narrow bracket strip that shows half spread and adverse selection as two adjoining
  brackets; below it a grey row with the class mean.
- **Panel B:** the same time axis in vol points, i.e. the implied vol of the fill against the curve.
- **Panel C:** raw against delta-neutral markout, the difference as a hatched area. This is the only place where
  delta-neutral is explained.
- **Pitfall:** a single case looks like evidence. The countermeasure is the p25/p75 band and the row with the class
  mean. No conversion note between vol points and USDC in the plot area.
- **Test:** `hs` of the fill shown is positive, its markout negative, and the three panels share the x-axis.

#### T2: From half spread to net edge (two-column, 3.0 in)

- **Data:** `waterfall_components` on the analysis frame, intervals per component from the cluster bootstrap over
  taker wallets. Recomputed: half spread +15.7012, adverse selection -2.6506, maker fee -1.5617, maker rebate
  +0.5930, hedge -3.1495, sum +8.9325, identical to the directly computed net edge.
- **Panel A:** waterfall with seven bars, the subtotal markout (+13.0506) and the final total starting on the zero
  line. Decreases carry diagonal hatching. The hedge bar has a dotted outline because it is the only one that is not
  observed but modelled, per Addendum 2, item 2. A 95 percent bootstrap interval per bar.
- **Panel B:** distribution of the fee as a share of the premium, separately for maker and taker, with lines at 12.5
  and 100 percent. Recomputed: the maker pays nothing at all on 66.9 percent of fills, its median is 0.00 percent
  and its q95 11.54 percent. The taker pays a median of 2.59 percent of the premium, more than 12.5 percent on 19.2
  percent of fills and more than the entire premium on 2.40 percent of fills. These fills are small, a median of 0.1
  contracts, and pay 20.8 basis points of notional against 3.5 basis points overall.
- **Pitfall:** the medians are not additive; the median half spread is 1.21 against a mean of 15.70. The axis is
  therefore explicitly labelled mean per contract, and one line gives the median as a contrast.
- **Test:** the five components sum to the net edge; the hedge bar is the only one with a dotted outline.

#### F1: How to read the number (two-column, 2.6 in)

- **Data:** `mo_usd_30m`, `amount`, `notional`, `index_price`. Recomputed: median 0.98, five percent trimmed mean
  6.36, mean 13.05, contract-weighted 0.264 USDC per contract. As a share of the premium q25 -1.6 percent, median
  +1.1 percent, q75 +21.1 percent. Aggregate maker profit per underlying 3.67 / 3.68 / 3.20 million USDC at mean
  index prices that differ by a factor of 1,599.
- **Panel A:** distribution of the markout on a symlog axis with a shaded linear core, three location lines
  (median, five percent trimmed mean, mean). The axis title states that each decade has the same area.
- **Panel B:** three bars of almost equal length for the aggregate dollar profit per underlying, next to them the
  fill count and the mean index price. This is the proof that USDC per contract is a statement about contract sizes.
- **Panel C:** markout as a share of the premium, shown as a distribution with q25, median and q75.
- **Pitfall:** an unmarked symlog axis deceives. The linear core is shaded and labelled.
- **Test:** the three location measures are in the correct order and the three dollar totals differ from each other
  by less than 20 percent, while the index prices differ by more than a factor of one thousand.
