## What was corrected

1. In the tape, fee and rebate of the maker row are sums over the whole fill. The first version subtracted them
   from the markout per contract. Now they enter as the sum divided by the amount.
2. The per-notional map (F5 top, card S5) divided the net edge per contract by the notional of the whole
   fill. Now: edge of the fill divided by its notional, that is 10⁴ · NE / index.
3. The markout as a share of the premium (F1c, F2c) divided the markout per contract by the premium of the whole
   fill. Now: markout per contract divided by price.
4. In the text of section 4, H2, H3 and H4 were stated in the opposite direction to the pre-registration (H2
   "toxic" instead of "uninformed", H3 "raised the toxicity" instead of "more toxic before the listing", H4 "a net
   edge survives" instead of "positive in fewer than half and negative at the money"). The tests in the code
   always followed the pre-registration; the verdicts do not change as a result.
5. The sentence that every interval was drawn as often as its headline number did not hold for the error bars of
   the figures (999 and 249 draws respectively). Now: intervals in the text with B = 9,999, figures with 999 or
   fewer. The newly cited interval of the net edge (1.56 to 18.31) comes from `decomposition_b9999` in
   `results/p1_finding/fee_units.csv`; the T2 error bars show 1.46 to 18.43 (B = 999).

## SSRN: abstract field (English)

```
A market maker on an options book loses to counterparties who know something and earns from those who do
not, but on an anonymous venue the two can only be told apart by proxy. Derive settles on chain, so its
public tape names both wallets. On 603,940 fills in BTC, ETH and HYPE options between January 2024 and
September 2026, the maker's mean markout thirty minutes after the fill is +13.05 USDC per contract: a half
spread of +15.70 plus adverse selection of -2.65. Fees, rebates and delta hedging leave a net edge of
+9.58. The loss is extremely concentrated, with ten taker wallets carrying 90.5 per cent of it, and those
ten are almost the entirety of the two professional counterparty classes. Trade size and sweeps, the
standard proxies for toxic flow, explain none of it once instrument-by-day fixed effects are included. Four
hypotheses were registered before any markout was computed; three are rejected.

Revised 25 September 2026: the maker fee and rebate now enter the net edge per contract; the note on the
first page lists what changed.
```

## SSRN: revision description (English)

```
Revision of 25 September 2026 (first version 19 September 2026). The trade tape books the maker fee and
rebate as sums over the whole fill; the first version subtracted them from a markout measured per contract,
its basis-point map divided the per-contract net edge by the notional of the whole fill, and its markout as
a share of the premium mixed the same units. All three are corrected, as recorded in addendum 3 to the
pre-registration. The net edge is +9.58 instead of +8.93 USDC per contract, and against vault flow it turns
from -28.58 to +2.09; 74 of 97 cells (76.3 per cent, previously 49.5) carry a net edge with a positive 90 per
cent interval, so H4 is now rejected on both of its branches; the basis-point map is redrawn and the ranking
of the underlyings reverses. Markouts and the tests of H1 to H3 are unchanged. Section 4 now states H2 to H4
in the direction of the pre-registration.
```
